# FAQ

## How do I top up?

Open [Wallet](/wallet), choose an amount and a payment method (USDT, Alipay or WeChat Pay, depending on what is enabled), and pay in the window that opens. The balance is credited automatically once the payment is confirmed; you do not need to stay on the page. The minimum amount and any top-up bonus are shown on the Wallet page.

## My payment went through but the balance did not change

USDT payments are credited after the network confirms the transfer, which can take a few minutes. Alipay and WeChat Pay are usually credited within a minute. If the balance has still not changed after 30 minutes, contact support with the order number shown in [Wallet](/wallet).

## How am I billed?

Pay as you go. Each request is charged for the tokens it uses (or per call or per image for some models), at the prices on the [Pricing](/pricing) page. Your [usage logs](/usage-logs) show the cost of every request. There is no monthly fee.

## Do you issue invoices?

No. ADTY AI does not issue invoices or VAT receipts. The order history in [Wallet](/wallet) and the usage logs are the records we provide.

## Can I get a refund?

See the [Refund Policy](/refund-policy).

## Which models can I use?

Every model on the [Pricing](/pricing) page. Use the model name exactly as shown there. One key works for all of them.

## Can I use one key in several tools?

Yes, but it is easier to create one key per tool on the [API Keys](/keys) page. Then you can see each tool's usage separately and disable one without affecting the others.

## Is my data stored?

Your requests are passed to the model provider to generate the response. The usage logs in the console record the model, tokens and cost of each request. What is kept and for how long is described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

Join the [Affiliate Program](/affiliate): share your link and earn a commission on the top-ups of the people you invite.
